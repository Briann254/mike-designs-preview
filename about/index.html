About Mike Designs

Every brand deserves to be seenproperly.

karibu to the studio

We work with small businesses, startups, churches, event organisers, institutions and individuals to create visual identities and branded materials that help them communicate, connect and stand out.

Our services bring design and production together. We support clients with creative concepts, artwork preparation, print materials, branded apparel, promotional products and customised gifts.

Whether you need a single item, materials for an event, or coordinated merchandise for a team, our goal is to make the process straightforward: from understanding the brief and preparing the design to coordinating production and arranging delivery or collection.
