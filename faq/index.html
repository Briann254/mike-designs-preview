What does Mike Designs do?

We provide graphic design, printing, branded apparel, promotional merchandise and customised products for businesses, churches, events, organisations and individuals.

Can I order a custom product?

Yes. Send us the product idea, quantity, preferred specifications and deadline. We will confirm feasibility and prepare a quote.

Do I need to have my design ready?

No. Share your idea or a reference. We can advise on design support and any related fees before work begins.

Can you print my existing design or logo?

Yes, subject to file quality, product compatibility and production requirements. We may ask for a high-resolution or vector file.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Bulk and event orders are welcome. Price and turnaround depend on product, quantity, specifications and production capacity.

How do I get a price?

Send an enquiry with the product, quantity, specifications and deadline. We will confirm the details and send a quote.

How long does an order take?

It varies by product, quantity, artwork approval, material availability and production method. Your estimated timeline is confirmed with your quote.

Do you deliver?

Delivery or collection is arranged for each order. Ask for the available options and any transport charges.

Can I reorder a previous job?

Yes. Share your order reference or approved artwork and we will check the specifications and quote again.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. Options depend on the stage of design, approval, purchasing and production, and the agreed terms.
