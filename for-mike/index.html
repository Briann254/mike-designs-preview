What's a placeholder.

  • Photos are free Unsplash placeholders, mostly shot in Nairobi. Your own work replaces them.
  • Product pictures on the products page are drawn illustrations.
  • The logo is a temporary wordmark until you send your logo files.
  • WhatsApp and call buttons go to your number. The email is Brian's test inbox until your business email is set up.
  • Hours and service area are examples.
  • The six projects and their client names are invented samples.
  • The quote form doesn't send yet, and booking is a demo until it's linked to a calendar. Both are wired up in the build.
  • Privacy and terms pages are empty until you approve the wording.

Your next steps

What we need from you to go live.

1.Decide

Before the build starts
  • Show prices, starting prices, or quote-only?
  • Deposit percentage and when the balance is due
  • Delivery area, charges, and any free-delivery zone
  • How many design revisions are included
  • Who answers enquiries, and how fast

2.Send

This week
  • Official phone / WhatsApp number, email, social handles
  • Business hours and whether to show an address
  • Logo files (SVG or high-resolution PNG)
  • At least 20 real photos of finished work, plus close-ups
  • Which client names, logos and projects we may show

3.Set up with Brian

One 1-hour call
  • Domain name, e.g. mikedesigns.co.ke, in your name
  • HostPinnacle hosting plan with Node.js, in your name
  • Website dashboard (Sanity), email sending (Resend), and a Google Calendar booking page
  • Google Business Profile, Analytics and Search Console

4.Approve

Before launch
  • Privacy policy and terms
  • FAQ answers and any payment or delivery rules
  • Final check on your phone
