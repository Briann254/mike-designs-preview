How to order

Clear steps.No surprises.

Production only starts once you have approved the artwork and the agreed payment is received. You can follow every step online.

The process

Five steps from your idea to your hands.

  1. 1

    Tell us your idea

    Share the product, quantity, sizes, deadline, design files and how it will be used.

  2. 2

    Get a quote

    We confirm specifications, pricing, estimated turnaround and delivery or collection.

  3. 3

    Approve the artwork

    Review the proof carefully and confirm the final design before anything is produced.

  4. 4

    Production & quality check

    Your order is produced or coordinated with a production partner, then checked.

  5. 5

    Receive your order

    Collect or receive delivery, and keep your order reference for easy reorders.

Before you enquire

What to have ready.

The more of this you can share, the faster and more accurate your quote. Missing something? Send what you have and we'll ask.

Request a quote Track an order

Order slip

  1. What you need

    The product or service, and how it will be used.

  2. Quantity and sizes

    Total quantity, garment sizes, dimensions or colours if known.

  3. Your deadline

    The date you need it by. We confirm what is realistic with your quote.

  4. Artwork or ideas

    Logo files, references or a description.

  5. Delivery or collection

    Your town or area, so transport can be included in the quote.

  6. Budget (optional)

    A range helps us recommend the right product.

Payments, proofs, timing

The details that matter.

Can you print my existing design or logo?

Yes, subject to file quality, product compatibility and production requirements. We may ask for a high-resolution or vector file.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Bulk and event orders are welcome. Price and turnaround depend on product, quantity, specifications and production capacity.

How long does an order take?

It varies by product, quantity, artwork approval, material availability and production method. Your estimated timeline is confirmed with your quote.

Do you deliver?

Delivery or collection is arranged for each order. Ask for the available options and any transport charges.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. Options depend on the stage of design, approval, purchasing and production, and the agreed terms.
